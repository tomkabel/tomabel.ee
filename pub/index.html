AiTM resilience

The anatomy of modern session compromise

A reverse-proxy phishing framework such as Evilginx does not imitate the login page. It relays it. The victim’s browser holds a TLS session with the attacker’s host, and the attacker’s host holds a second one with the real identity provider. Every page, every one-time code and every push approval is genuine, forwarded in real time.

When the user completes MFA, the identity provider issues a session cookie, and the proxy in the middle keeps a copy. Replayed from the attacker’s browser, that cookie needs neither the password nor the second factor. The session is already authenticated.

The controls that hold bind authentication to something a relay cannot forward: the origin the browser actually connected to (FIDO2 / WebAuthn), a key that never leaves the device (DBSC, token binding), or a network fingerprint that disagrees with the client it claims to be. ProksiAbel OÜ knows where each of these holds and where it leaks, because its founder built and tuned the relays they exist to stop.

  • Legacy MFA assumptions

    A second factor proves the right person is present.

    AiTM proxy reality

    The proxy relays the prompt and keeps the session cookie the factor produced. Presence was proven, for the attacker’s benefit.

  • Standard WAF rules

    Block on IP reputation, request rate and known signatures.

    TLS & behavioral fingerprinting

    A relay speaks with its own TLS stack. JA3 / JA4, HTTP/2 settings and header order contradict the browser its User-Agent names.

The full account, in his words→

Tom Kristian Abel did not learn session hijacking from a textbook. He spent years reverse engineering browser security, TLS fingerprinting and anti-fraud systems, and for part of that time he built and sold a modified version of the Evilginx reverse-proxy toolkit. On 20 November 2024 Harju County Court convicted him of preparing computer crime. He served the custodial part of the sentence and takes full responsibility for it.

What that work left behind is ground truth: an exact, practical understanding of how an adversary-in-the-middle relay is built, tuned and hidden, and of which controls actually stop it. That knowledge now works only for defenders. He publishes his research, reports findings through coordinated disclosure, including to SK ID Solutions and RIA, and takes engagements solely under signed rules of engagement.
